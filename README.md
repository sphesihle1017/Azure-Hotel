# Customer Features

## 👤 Customer Dashboard

![Customer Dashboard](Screenshots/Screenshot%202026-08-03%20114914.png)

The Customer Dashboard allows guests to browse available rooms, manage their profile, view booking history, and quickly make new reservations from a single interface.

---

## 📅 Room Booking

![Room Booking](Screenshots/Screenshot%202026-08-03%20115251.png)

Customers can reserve rooms by selecting their check-in and check-out dates. The system automatically calculates the number of nights, booking cost, and verifies room availability before confirming the reservation.

---

## 📄 Booking Details

![Booking Details](Screenshots/Screenshot%202026-08-03%20115024.png)

After a successful reservation, customers can review a complete booking summary, including guest information, hotel details, room type, booking status, and payment information.

---

## 🧾 Booking Receipt

![Booking Receipt](Screenshots/Screenshot%202026-08-03%20114837.png)

The booking receipt provides a printable confirmation containing reservation details, payment summary, and important hotel information such as check-in and check-out times.
